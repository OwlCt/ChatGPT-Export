# Code Of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation from everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about technical issues.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Give clear reproduction steps when reporting bugs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.
- Attempts to add tracking, data exfiltration, or deceptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participating in the project.
